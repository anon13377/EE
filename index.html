Get your edits fast. Everything else follows.

Building matters because it's how you protect yourself. If you can't build, you die — that's it. This isn't a lecture, it's drills. Run these and your edits come out quicker, cleaner, and you stop being the one who's slow when it matters.

Start Here

5 Mistakes You're Making

Before the drills — here's what's actually costing you fights.

01

Using an SMG on someone far away

Switch your weapon for the range. An SMG at distance is just wasted bullets.

02

Not saving shockwaves and grapplers

Everyone pops them for no reason. Save them for when you actually need the escape or the reposition.

03

Hiding behind trees instead of building

A tree isn't cover you control. Build it instead — you decide when it breaks, not them.

04

Placing cones wrong so your avatar shows

Half your body sticking out of a cone gives away exactly where you are — and ranked players will punish it every time.

05

Going for a 75% open edit

That's basically no edit at all. You're standing in the open and calling it protection.

Signature Move

Drop Down

The go-to move — get height, take the fight from above, get back down clean.

The Play

Drop Down

Situation: you need high ground, or you already have it and someone's below you
  • 1 Box up first — 4 walls, that's your protection before anything else.
  • 2 Build stairs and walls on each side to climb up to height.
  • 3 Add floors and a box at the top once you're up.
  • 4 Edit the cone downward at the peak so you can see and hit whoever's below.
  • 5 Edit the stairs to drop back down fast when you need to reposition.
Why it works: editing down on people below means you can actually hit your shots on them — you're seeing them from above instead of guessing through a wall. And having the stairs edited means you're never stuck at the top; you can get down just as fast as you got up.

The Play

Get The Shot

Situation: someone's close and rushing, or peeking from a distance
  • 1 Someone rushing in close? Window edit — see them and shoot fast.
  • 2 Someone further away? Half wall — peek and shoot without fully exposing.
  • 3 Never a door. It's window or half wall, depending on the distance.
Why it works: the edit you pick should match the range, not just be whatever's fastest. Window up close, half wall from range — that's the whole decision.

Practice

The Drills

Run these on repeat. You'll know it's working when edits come out quicker, you're landing more shots right after the edit, and you stop dying in fights you used to lose.

1

Grid Drill

The secret one: it's a 3x3 grid on the piece — press everything but the tool in the bottom left or right corner and you get a clean edit. Do this on repeat until it's automatic.

Fixes: raw edit speedImproving when: edits come out quicker, no hesitation
2

Box & Reset

Box up with 4 walls. Reset your build back to normal when nobody's close, so nobody can sneak up on you. Then practice editing out fast when you're getting shot at.

Fixes: close combat, surviving box fightsImproving when: you're not dying as much in your box
3

Drop Down

Practice the full sequence: box, stairs up, cone edit downward at the top, then edit the stairs to get back down fast.

Fixes: reading your location vs your opponent'sImproving when: more kills off height, cleaner rotations down
4

Wall Edit Drill

Practice reading the range and picking window vs half wall instantly — no door, ever. React to distance, not habit.

Fixes: different edit patterns for different fightsImproving when: you're hitting shots right after the edit instead of getting caught
5

Piece Control

The mini-game. Just reps — run it to keep every edit type sharp, not just your favorite one.

Fixes: overall edit speed and varietyImproving when: edits are strong across the board, more kills, less panic
